# Negative pp deltas (`q4km-96k`, `m128-prof`): why they are not readable and what the journal now carries

Reference machine: RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB, 32 GiB RAM, i7-4790K. Measurements by `bench_dialog.py` (llama
path), carriers of 04.11 and 17.11, re-shot 18.11 by the stock measure. The executive runbook with
the unfreeze variants is `docs/plan/2026-11-18-q4km-pp-drop.md` (RU) and `.en.md`; this note is the
condensed "what has been measured" part.

## Why

In the 17.11 digest four rows have a negative pp delta and two of them looked like a regression of
accepted code. None of them can be checked against the 04.11 and 17.11 carriers: a measurement row of
those days carries only `tag/turn/sha/prompt_n/cached_n/pp/tg/n_predict` — neither the engine head,
nor the actual `args`, nor the model volume. Since 18.11 the journal carries the run carrier, and
that is exactly what separates a future pair from the old one.

## Why "not readable" rather than "regression" or "noise"

1. In `q4km` the same turn 1 flips direction: pp −19.76 %, tg +18.03 % (18.374 → 21.686). The pair
   shows no one-directional slowdown of code.
2. `m128-prof` keeps the same bits also on turn 2 (`a4fc7e1912c3c513`), yet turn-2 pp drops by 15.2 %
   (25.66 against 30.25) — there is nothing left to compare by prompt composition, only the state of
   the run differs.
3. `m128-up-1` on the same input returns to the baseline within 0.02 % — the baseline is reproducible.
4. Both q4km pp rows sit two orders of magnitude below the SSD baseline of the same window (928.1):
   `Q4_K_M` 19.78 GiB lives on the `G:` HDD, so these are disk numbers, not engine numbers.
5. In the recorded conditions of the pair the `ncmoe` lever is held differently for different quants
   — the 04.11 note («кстати, пока начинаешь…», lines 36, 247, 231) gives q4km at `ncmoe 30,
   ub 4096` and the Q5_K_S master control at `ncmoe 32` (800 tok/s where Q4_K_M master gives 277).
   Without `args` in the measurement row one compares the layout, not the code.

## The carrier of the measurement since 18.11

The user's order of 18.11 — «с hdd замеры не снимать» (do not take measurements from HDD) — is
enforced by code: the preflight at `docs/scripts/research/bench_dialog.py:99` calls `disk_of()` from
`engine/packaging/kit/detect.py:211` before the server starts and exits with `rc=3`, printing `drive`
and `media`, when `media != "SSD"`. An unknown volume type (`media=None`) is blocked on the same
ground. The accepted gate of 18.11: a model on `G:` gave `rc=3`, `drive=G: media=HDD (ATA WDC
WD3200BPVT-8)`, and the server was never started.

Every new journal row carries eight carrier fields — `exe`, `args`, `model_path`, `disk` (volume,
`media`, `free_gib`, `model`, `bus`), `env`, `engine_commit`, `llama_commit`, `ram_free_gib` (record
assembly: `docs/scripts/research/bench_dialog.py:143`). The names are the same ones already used by
the capture profiles `E:/tmp/grid16/grid16-*.json`, so a run delta and a capture profile are described
by one set of fields. The completeness measure is `docs/scripts/research/series_report.py:519`
(`--need-carrier`, rc=1 if some row has an unmeasured field; a `disk` without `media` counts as
unmeasured).

Volumes under the stand (measured 18.11 by `disk_of()`): `C:` SSD ATA Gigastone Prime, 52.3 GiB
free; `D:` SSD ATA TOSHIBA THNSNK12, 14.1 GiB; `E:` HDD TOSHIBA MK6476GS, 9.9 GiB; `G:` HDD WDC
WD3200BPVT-8, 59.3 GiB. Nine GGUF on the stand: five on SSD (23.62 / 23.06 / 23.04 / 17.86 / 17.44
GiB), four on HDD (including `Q4_K_M` 19.78 GiB on `G:`). The journal and `master.txt` on `E:` do not
violate the order: they are not the measured carrier — `master.txt` is read once before the server
starts, and journal writes happen outside the measured region and describe the weights volume through
the `disk` field.

## What follows from this

The rows `q4km-96k-old/master`, `m128-prof`, `fix29941-96k`, `fixes-96k` in the digest must read
"not interpretable: the run carrier was not recorded" — not "regression" and not "noise";
`q4km-96k-old → master` enters the speed-up rating with no sign at all. Unfreezing requires a run
from SSD: the "old side" build does not exist on the stand (the oldest live `llama-server.exe` is
from 12.11, the 04.11 head is below the shallow history bottom `2bc46baa9`), and capturing from `G:`
is forbidden. The variants and their prices are in the runbook, §3.
